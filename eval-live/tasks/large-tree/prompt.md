Our customers have moved to a new standard box, so the line between bulky parcels and the rest moves: a parcel is now
bulky when its longest side is over 120 cm (it was over 100 cm). A parcel up to 120 cm must be handled as an ordinary
one everywhere the shop treats bulky parcels differently: what we charge for it, which carrier takes it, what is printed
on its label, and how the weekly report counts it.

The postal service's own size limit and the customs rules come from a contract and from law, not from us: they do not
change. Keep the tests passing and bring the ones that encode the old line up to date.
